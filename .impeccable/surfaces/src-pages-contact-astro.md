# Surface brief: /contact/

Scope: src/pages/contact.astro. Visitor mode: Persuade — this page is the conversion, and the end of the journey.

Audience & job: someone who has decided to reach out and now has to write the first message to a stranger about an expensive problem. The page's job is to remove every excuse not to send it.

Structural rule: this is the only page with no closing navy band. It is the ending; there is nowhere onward to point.

Composition: page header (copper label, "Tell us what you're up against", lede that explicitly permits a "no"), then a two-column body — the email block on a copper-washed panel (address at display size, copy-address button with aria-live confirmation) beside a "Helpful things to mention" list that lowers the blank-page cost of writing.

Conversion constraints, all binding and hard-won from earlier critique findings: there is deliberately no contact form (a product decision — static site, no backend). Every mailto carries a `?subject=` prefill. The plain-text address must be selectable and reachable without a working mail client, which is why the copy button exists and why the address also appears in the footer of every page and in the home page hero.

Tone: the lede promises an honest answer including "no". That reassurance is the page's real conversion mechanism — it is not decoration and should not be cut for brevity.
