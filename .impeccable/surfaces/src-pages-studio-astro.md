# Surface brief: /studio/

Scope: src/pages/studio.astro. Visitor mode: Persuade, with a Read register — this is where a cautious buyer decides whether the people behind the work are credible.

Audience & job: a business owner who has read the claim and wants to know who they'd actually be dealing with, and how the engagement runs. The page answers "who" and "how", then hands off to /contact/.

Content constraints: the studio is one person presented as "we". Derrick Wengerd is named here and only here — the one place the site drops the company voice for a human. No fabricated team, no invented headcount, no client names.

Composition: page header ("Small on purpose"), then the founder block on a copper-washed panel with a navy monogram, then the three-step method as one bordered object split into three panels, then the navy band to /contact/.

The method block is the page's signature: the panels' top bars run copper → copper/navy gradient → navy, making "listening becomes building" visible. It is the only gradient permitted in the design system, and the only numbered sequence on the site (numbering is legitimate because the method genuinely is a sequence).

Unresolved: whether to add a real photograph of the founder in place of the monogram — likely a credibility gain, but it needs a good photo, and a bad one costs more than the monogram does.
